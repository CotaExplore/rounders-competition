Competition rules

Bowling. The ball must be bowled underarm only. Overarm bowling is not allowed.

Running. Runners go around the outside of the posts, following the running line. Only one runner is allowed at each post at a time. Runners must not overtake the player ahead of them, including where that player is held up or waiting at a post.

Scoring. A batter scores one rounder for hitting the ball and running the full circuit back to the start. A batter scores half a rounder for hitting the ball and reaching second or third post, or for running the full circuit without having hit the ball.

Out. A batter or runner is out if: the ball is caught before it touches the ground; the post they are running to is stumped (a fielder holds the ball at that post) before they reach it; they overtake another runner; or they run inside the posts instead of around the outside.

No contact. There must be no deliberate physical contact between players. Any player who makes deliberate contact is disqualified from that game. If three players from the same team are disqualified in one game, the whole team is disqualified from that game.

Weaker hand (fair play handicap). To keep batting fair between age groups, an older batter must bat with their weaker hand (a right-handed player bats left-handed, and a left-handed player bats right-handed) in these situations: a Senior batting against a Company or Junior bowler; a Leader batting against a Senior, Company or Junior bowler; and a Company member batting against a Junior bowler. In all other cases players bat with their normal hand.

Leader participation

Batting. Leaders may bat, but they do not run. When a Leader bats, a Senior or Company member is designated to run the circuit on their behalf.

Fielding. Leaders may field or bowl, but they cannot field on a post or base, and cannot stand in the running line, so that the running route stays clear.

Posts and bases. All post and base positions are taken by Seniors or Company members, not by Leaders.

Umpires. Leaders must not override an umpire's decision, except on safety grounds, where a Leader may always step in.

Disputes. Any dispute over scoring or judging should be brought to the Centre Warden.

Spirit of the game. Being competitive is good, but this competition is for fun. Please play, and support, in that spirit.

Umpiring

Three umpires. Each game has three umpires: one from 6th Doncaster, one from 82nd Belfast and one from 2nd Rochdale.

Decisions. Where a decision is disputed, the three umpires vote on it and the majority decision is final. If the dispute concerns scoring or judging, it should be brought to the Centre Warden rather than argued on the pitch.

Safeguarding

A no-contact sport. This is a no-contact competition. Players of all ages and abilities are welcome to take part, but there must be no physical contact between players at any time. Everyone is asked to play safely and look after one another on the pitch.

Names and records. The online scoring and fixtures system records team results only. No individual player, young person or Leader names are stored against teams, fixtures or scores at any point.

First aid and incidents

First aid. First aid is provided by Group First Aiders. At least one Group First Aider and a minimum of two Officers must be present at each game. The Centre Warden provides backup.

Incidents. All incidents, injuries or near-misses must be reported to the Centre Warden as soon as possible so they can be recorded and acted on.
